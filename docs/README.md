# Docs

What the code can't tell you: what we measured, what we found, and why
things are the way they are. How something is built belongs in the code and
its comments; what was built when is in git history. No roadmaps or build
logs here: when a finding changes, change the doc that holds it.

| Doc | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [costs.md](costs.md) | What running TinyFloor costs: measured media rates, what an office costs a month, the margin at $29, and the guardrails that protect it |
| [calls.md](calls.md) | What testing showed about calls and meetings: always relayed, UDP first, voice sent twice, audio first, why not Google Meet |
| [pricing.md](pricing.md) | Why Free for 3 and one $29 plan: who buys, what others charge, the trial, fair use, and how the price is said |
| [billing.md](billing.md) | Who takes the money (Creem, as an individual in India), why not Paddle, Creem's quirks, and the Indian tax side |
| [product.md](product.md) | What keeps a team on the floor, what we deliberately don't build, and small product decisions worth remembering |
| [customization.md](customization.md) | What the art packs hold and where the files live, what items, layouts and characters need to be, and what trying them in the lab showed |
| [analytics.md](analytics.md) | What we measure in PostHog and why, what recordings hide, and how test traffic is kept out |
| [search.md](search.md) | What search engines and AI assistants actually did with us, and the rules for comparison pages |
| [sites.md](sites.md) | The site and the app as two Workers: links that must keep working, and the build gotchas |
| [development.md](development.md) | Known debt: adding pages and languages without gaps, pages still to decide, what waits on search engines |
